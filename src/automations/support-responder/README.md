# Support Responder
Auto-reply plus triage for incoming support messages.

## Problem
Support requests pile up outside business hours.

## Solution
Auto-reply with category detection and queueing for human follow-up.

## Inputs
- support channel or webhook
- categories
- business hours

## Outputs
- reply draft
- ticket status
- escalation flag

## Acceptance Criteria
- Instant acknowledgment
- Accurate category tagging
- Escalation rules visible in runbook
