# Invoice Bot
Automated invoicing and payment reminders.

## Problem
Manual invoicing and follow-ups are time-consuming and often delayed.

## Solution
Schedule invoices and reminders automatically using business rules.

## Inputs
- customer email
- invoice amount
- due date

## Outputs
- sent invoice
- reminder log

## Acceptance Criteria
- Invoice reaches inbox on time
- Reminder respects business hours
- Opt-out honored immediately
